Family/pathway \"edges\" (Pon1\u2013Pon2\u2013Pon3; the cholesterol pathway Hmgcr\u2013Sqle\u2013Lss) are functional/co-expression associations, not physical binding, and should be placed in a separate functional-association tier.\n- **Statistical rigor:** Effect sizes and FDR were not in the raw outputs; hub confidence currently rests on cross-dataset recurrence. Recommended: re-extract FDR-adjusted p-values, \u0394\u03b2, and log2FC; filter to robust nodes; apply degree-preserving permutation null models and bootstrap hub-centrality tests.\n- **Directionality:** Methylation appears upstream of expression for some hubs (Fkbp5 is the clearest causal case; Ugt1a cluster likely concordant). Gene-body methylation (common in MBD-seq hits) may correlate positively with expression, so inverse logic must be applied cautiously with probe-location annotation. Integrating TF\u2192target regulons (NRF2\u2192Nqo1/Ugt/Gst; NF-\u03baB\u2192Tnf/chemokines; GR/Fkbp5 feedback) would convert undirected edges into a directed causal graph.\n- **Temporal dynamics:** The transcriptome exists only at the acute (8-day) time point; 12/43-week data are epigenetic only. Tnf and the Pon/Nrf2 axis appear as initiators; Fkbp5-driven reprogramming and myelin/lipid modules sustain chronicity.\n- **BXD genetic architecture:** Pon1, Fkbp5, and the H2 haplotype segregate between B6 and D2 with strong cis-regulation; overlaying GeneNetwork BXD eQTL/mQTL and classifying cis vs. trans would explain differential strain susceptibility.\n- **Tissue specificity:** Cross-tissue edges (CNS modules vs. hepatic Pon/Ugt/Cyp) may be artifacts if profiled tissues differ; anatomically implausible edges should be pruned.\n- **Sex as a biological variable:** X-linked/sex genes (Xlr, Nxf7, Sly, Mageb18, Ighm) indicate sex may not be fully controlled; sex should be modeled as a covariate.\n- **Data quality:** Predicted Gm/Rik models, pseudogenes, miRNAs, and olfactory/vomeronasal receptors should be down-weighted or held in a low-confidence tier.\n- **Human translation:** Strongest orthologous, blood/CSF-accessible candidates are PON1 (activity/Q192R variant, veteran-cohort support), FKBP5 (methylation/stress-disorder biomarker), and TNF/IL6 cytokine panels. Druggable hubs with existing compounds: NLRP3 (MCC950), TNF (etanercept), FKBP5 (SAFit2), NRF2 activators (sulforaphane, dimethyl fumarate), Cox-2/Ptgs2 inhibitors, and microglial inhibitors (minocycline).'"
