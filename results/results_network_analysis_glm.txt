Consensus mechanistic model \u2014 how corticosterone + DFP produce Gulf War disease\n\n1. **Dual exposure:** corticosterone (glucocorticoid stress hormone) + diisopropylfluorophosphate (organophosphate acetylcholinesterase inhibitor).\n2. **Cholinergic hit:** DFP inhibits acetylcholinesterase \u2192 acetylcholine accumulation \u2192 overstimulation of muscarinic (Chrm3) and nicotinic (Chrna10) receptors; Bche provides endogenous OP scavenging; **Chrna10 methylation at 43 weeks locks in cholinergic remodeling** (autonomic, CNS and GI effects).\n3. **Glucocorticoid programming:** corticosterone engages the GR chaperone complex (**Fkbp5\u2013Hspa1a/Hspa1b**); Fkbp5 induction (itself differentially methylated in S1 and induced in S4) reduces GR sensitivity \u2192 impaired HPA negative feedback; **Hsd11b1** regenerates active glucocorticoid intracellularly \u2192 chronic glucocorticoid amplification.\n4. **Detoxification bottleneck:** clearance of DFP and steroids depends on **Pon1/2/3, Bche, the Ugt1a glucuronidation program, Cyp/Sult/Slco phase I\u2013II enzymes, and Nqo1/Gsto2/Hmox1 redox detox**; strain-dependent capacity gates susceptibility, and methylation of the Ugt1a program creates a persistent detoxification deficit (chemical sensitivity).\n5. **Oxidative stress:** residual toxicant plus cholinergic stress generate ROS (**Cybb/NOX2**) that overwhelm the antioxidant cohort (Nqo1, Hmox1, Cat, Srxn1, Txnrd1, Gsto2, Gstm1, Gstk1) \u2192 cumulative oxidative tissue damage.\n6. **Innate immune activation:** sensing via the **Tlr1:Tlr2 heterodimer** (Tlr1 methylated at 12 weeks), Tlr9, and Cd14 \u2192 NF-\u03baB \u2192 **Tnf, Il1a, Il1b, Il6, Il21, Il23a, Il33** + chemokines (Ccl2, Ccl3, Ccl4, Cxcl10\u2026); the **Nlrp3\u2013Casp4/Casp12 inflammasome** matures Il1b; **Il6/Lif \u2192 JAK \u2192 Stat3** and **Myc** amplify the program; Mmp9 and adhesion molecules (Icam1, Vcam1, Sele, Sell) execute leukocyte trafficking; the chr17 **H2/MHC island** co-activates.\n7. **Failed counter-regulation:** the brakes **Socs3** (STAT3 feedback) and **Zc3h12c** (Regnase RNase destabilizing Il6/Il1b mRNA) are engaged but insufficient \u2014 **methylation of Zc3h12c at 43 weeks disables a key inflammation brake**, yielding chronic low-grade neuroinflammation.\n8. **Stress-response consolidation:** Ddit3, Xbp1 and the convergent Wfs1 node are part of the stress-response program induced by the combined inflammatory/glucocorticoid load.\n9. **Epigenetic lock-in:** differential methylation concentrates on precisely the loci controlling the exposure-response axes \u2014 GR sensitivity (**Fkbp5**), detoxification (**Ugt1a6a/7c/9, Gsto2, Sult3a2, Slco1a6, Cyp2u1**), innate sensing (**Tlr1**), cholinergic tone (**Chrna10**), inflammation braking (**Zc3h12c**) \u2014 and includes **Nsun2** (RNA methyltransferase, itself methylated at 43 weeks), consistent with feed-forward epigenetic consolidation that endures to 43 weeks.\n10. **Neural effectors:** convergent methylation+expression genes in neural/structural/transport functions (Fam107a, Col28a1, Dock1, Phactr3, Gng10, Ndrg1, Ncam1, Slc1a3, Slc44a4), together with cholinergic (Chrm3, Chrna10) and dopaminergic/glutamatergic modulation (Drd2/Drd4, Grm3), commit the injury to the CNS/PNS.\n11. **Conclusion (consensus):** Gulf War disease emerges as a **chronic, self-sustaining state driven by self-reinforcing loops between the glucocorticoid/HPA module (Fkbp5\u2013Hspa1a/b\u2013Hsd11b1) and the neuroinflammatory module (Tnf\u2013Il6\u2013Stat3\u2013Nlrp3)** \u2014 initiated by cholinergic/organophosphate toxicity (Bche\u2013Chrm3\u2013Chrna10), gated by detoxification capacity (Pon1/2/3, Ugt1a, Akr1c14, Nqo1), amplified by oxidative stress (Cybb vs. Nqo1/Hmox1/Cat), and locked in by persistent methylation of regulatory loci.\n\n---\n\n## 7.
